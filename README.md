# Meghdrishti City Watch

*"Build a modern, dark-mode responsive web dashboard called 'AquaPulse India' for tracking heavy rainfall and urban flood risks. Include:

A top header with a live simulated weather status for an Indian city (e.g., Mumbai/Jaipur), temperature, and a 'Severe Weather Alert' banner.

4 metric cards: Current Rainfall (mm/hr), Soil Saturation, Drainage Capacity, and AI Risk Index (Moderate/High).

An interactive visual section acting as a city map layout with clickable zones (e.g., North District - Safe, Central Zone - Flood Warning, Low-lying Area - Critical Risk).

A panel showing 'AI Predictive Forecast' for the next 6 hours with a timeline graph or progress bars.

An 'Emergency Preparedness & Action Guide' section with practical tips for citizens and local authorities."*
